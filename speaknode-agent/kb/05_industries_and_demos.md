SpeakNode is the AI receptionist designed for that math. It answers every inbound call, books or reschedules the appointment, captures intake, and writes the result into your scheduling system — across all your locations, 24/7.

## The missed-call math
Each location takes roughly 80 to 250 inbound calls per week.

At an average $600 per booked visit, a 25-location group loses high five figures per month to dropped calls alone.
An always-on AI receptionist catches the overflow and the after-hours tail — the calls a human front desk physically cannot.
