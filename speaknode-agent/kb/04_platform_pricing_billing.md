## Billing
Usage-based billing and subscription management in Speaknode
Speaknode uses usage-based billing powered by GetLago.

## Billing Model
Billing is based on voice minutes — the actual time spent in conversations with AI agents.

## Subscriptions & Plans
Each space has a subscription plan that defines:
Included voice minutes per billing period
Overage pricing
Feature access levels
Agent limits

## Usage Tracking
The platform tracks usage automatically:
Every agent session records start and end time
Duration is calculated and reported to GetLago
GetLago aggregates usage against the plan
Invoices are generated at the end of the billing period

## Payment Processing
Payments flow through the platform's billing engine, which integrates with a configurable secure payment provider:
Automatic payment method sync
Invoice generation and delivery
Overage billing

## Billing Management
In the admin panel:
View current usage and remaining minutes
Manage subscription plan
View invoice history
Update payment method

## Billing & Subscriptions
Managing subscriptions, usage, invoices, and payments in Speaknode
Speaknode uses a subscription-based billing model with usage-based pricing for voice minutes.

## Viewing Your Current Plan
Navigate to Settings > Billing.
Your current plan is displayed at the top of the page, showing:
Plan Name: The name of your active subscription.
Included Minutes: The number of voice minutes included in your plan per billing period.
Price: The monthly or annual cost.

## Usage
The billing page shows your current usage for the billing period:
Voice Minutes Used: The total minutes consumed across all conversations (inbound, outbound, and widget sessions).
Minutes Remaining: How many included minutes are left before overage billing begins.
Usage Breakdown: A chart or table showing usage over time.
Usage resets at the start of each billing period. Unused minutes do not roll over.

## Changing Your Plan
Navigate to Settings > Billing.
Click Change Plan.
Review the available plans and their features.
Select the plan you want to switch to.
Confirm the change.
Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.

## Invoices
Navigate to Settings > Billing.
Scroll down to the Invoices section.
View past invoices with dates, amounts, and status (paid, pending).
Click on any invoice to download a PDF.

## Payment Method
Navigate to Settings > Billing.
In the Payment Method section, click Update to add or change your credit card.
You will be redirected to a secure checkout page hosted by the payment provider.
Enter your card details and confirm.
Major credit and debit cards are supported. Your card details are never stored on Speaknode servers — they are handled by the payment provider directly.

## Overage Billing
If you exceed your plan's included voice minutes, additional minutes are billed at the overage rate specified in your plan.
Overage charges are calculated at the end of the billing period.
They appear as a separate line item on your invoice.
You will receive an email notification when you reach 80% and 100% of your included minutes.
If you frequently exceed your included minutes, consider upgrading to a higher plan to get a better per-minute rate.
